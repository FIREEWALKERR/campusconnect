How we measure placement

Eligible cohort = enabled student accounts with department, graduation year, CGPA and backlog count completed, optionally filtered by graduation batch. This is reporting eligibility, not eligibility for every job. Placed = distinct students in that cohort with an accepted offer matching the selected company and drive academic year. Percentage = placed ÷ eligible × 100; an empty cohort returns 0%. Company and academic-year filters restrict outcomes, not the cohort denominator. Salary summaries include all issued offers in the filtered set; no offers is shown as 0.
